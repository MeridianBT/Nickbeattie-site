Integration

Integrating Australia and New Zealand

Two countries, six divisions, one operating model.

$1B revenue · 250+ people · 6 divisions

The situation
Honda's Australian and New Zealand auto operations run separately. The aim is to create value, strengthen governance and grow people.
What I did
Set up the program: executive engagement, steering committee, PMO and current-state analysis. Set the vision, goals, targets and principles. Now designing the target operating model, functional structure and tax structure. Stepped in as interim Head of Finance in New Zealand and built a channel P&L that's now used for decisions on channel mix, pricing, incentives and dealer management.
What changed
In design. Targets include G&A savings, a consolidated board and leadership team, right-sized headcount, tighter governance, and better career paths and talent depth across both countries.
